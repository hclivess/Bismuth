Bismuth AMM — constant-product (x·y=k) BIS ⇄ token pool (VM demo)

The pool is always a counterparty: swaps execute instantly at a slippage price off R_bis · R_tok = k, and the 0.3% fee on every swap stays in the pool — that growing k is the liquidity providers' yield. Trustless: the contract escrows both reserves, no operator. This is a demo (32-bit reserves capped near 10.7 BIS/side; honest impermanent-loss caveat in doc/24 §3).
