Bismuth Poker — heads-up Texas Hold'em (VM demo)

The chain is the trustless referee: it escrows the pot, anchors your hole-card commitments, and settles the showdown on-chain (verifying your cards and evaluating the best hand). Hole cards stay private via an off-chain mental-poker deal (a cooperative commutative-cipher shuffle between the two browsers — secret keys never leave your machine). This demo is heads-up all-in (stake, then play or fold); see doc/24 §4 for the honest limits.
