[center][size=26pt][b][color=royalblue]B I S M U T H[/color] [color=purple](BIS)[/color][/b][/size][/center]
[center][size=12pt][i]A fair-launched, from-scratch Python blockchain — no ICO, no premine — live on mainnet since 1 May 2017. Widely cited as the first blockchain written in Python — not a fork.[/i][/size][/center]

[hr]
[center][size=20pt][b][color=purple]⚡ UPCOMING HARD FORK — "hf2"[/color][/b][/size][/center]

[center][size=13pt][b][color=red]MINERS & POOLS: ACTION REQUIRED.[/color][/b] hf2 modernises the Proof-of-Work (sha224 → [b]blake2b Heavy3[/b]). After activation, nodes and miners running old software will produce [b]invalid blocks[/b]. Update to the latest node + mining software [b]before[/b] the fork.[/size][/center]

[size=11pt][/size]
[b]hf2 is a single bundled hard fork that brings:[/b]
[list]
[li][b]Modernised PoW[/b] — blake2b Heavy3 replaces sha224 (dual-algo during transition).[/li]
[li][b]Binary / integer serialization[/b] — smaller, faster, deterministic transactions and blocks.[/li]
[li][b]Coinbase upgrades[/b] — free-form coinbase fields, [b]coinbase maturity[/b] (block rewards become spendable only after they're buried, BTC-style), and [b]multi-scheme mining[/b] (RSA, ECDSA, ED25519, multisig and ML-DSA addresses can all mine — not just RSA).[/li]
[li][b]Privacy[/b] — opt-in shielded transactions: stealth addresses, ring signatures, and confidential amounts (RingCT).[/li]
[li][b]Smart contracts[/b] — an on-chain RISC-V (RV32I) virtual machine and a growing set of dApps.[/li]
[li][b]Miner accountability[/b] — equivocation-slashing groundwork (the "accuser").[/li]
[/list]
[b]Activation is miner-signalled[/b] (version-bits style): upgraded miners stamp "hf2" in their coinbase, and the fork locks in once a full window of blocks signals. [b]Signal readiness by updating now.[/b]

[center][size=20pt][b][color=purple]10 Years of Bismuth — Architectural Firsts (2017–2026)[/color][/b][/size][/center]
[center][size=11pt]Nine years on mainnet and counting — a from-scratch chain that shipped ideas years before they had names:[/size][/center]
[list]
[li][b]The first blockchain written in Python[/b] — built from scratch, not a fork.[/li]
[li][b]Semantic data / "meaning" layering[/b] — structured, layered on-chain data from the start.[/li]
[li][b]An early plugin ecosystem[/b] — tokens, aliases, NFTs, a DEX, naming, and state channels as modular plugins.[/li]
[li][b]On-chain sensor data[/b] — years before the industry coined "DePIN".[/li]
[li][b]A peer-reviewed PoW difficulty controller[/b] — consensus analysis published in [i]Modeling, Identification and Control[/i] (2017–2018).[/li]
[/list]
[center][size=11pt]hf2 carries that tradition forward — adding on-chain privacy and smart contracts to a chain that has run, uninterrupted and community-owned, for nearly a decade.[/size][/center]
