[center][size=22pt][b][color=purple]⚡ hf2 — the biggest upgrade in Bismuth's history[/color][/b][/size][/center]
[center][size=12pt]One hard fork, bundling a decade of modernization:[/size][/center]

[b][color=blue]•[/color] Privacy[/b] — opt-in shielded transactions: stealth addresses, ring signatures, and confidential amounts (RingCT).
[b][color=blue]•[/color] Smart contracts[/b] — a real on-chain RISC-V (RV32I) virtual machine, with a growing set of dApps.
[b][color=blue]•[/color] Modernized PoW[/b] — blake2b Heavy3 replaces sha224 (dual-algo through the transition).
[b][color=blue]•[/color] Lean serialization[/b] — native binary/integer transactions and blocks: smaller, faster, deterministic.
[b][color=blue]•[/color] Coinbase overhaul[/b] — free-form fields, BTC-style coinbase maturity, and multi-scheme mining (RSA, ECDSA, ED25519, multisig, and post-quantum ML-DSA can all mine).

[center][size=13pt][b][color=red]⛏ MINERS & POOLS — ACTION REQUIRED:[/color][/b] hf2 switches the PoW to [b]blake2b Heavy3[/b]. Old miners will produce [b]invalid blocks[/b] after activation — update before the fork. Activation is [b]miner-signalled[/b]: upgraded miners stamp "hf2" and the fork locks in once the network signals.[/size][/center]
